## Platform Capabilities

- **Market intelligence:** market scanning, multi-symbol monitoring, research and cross-market analysis.
- **Execution infrastructure:** broker/exchange connectivity and venue-selection architecture.
- **Investment Management:** portfolio visibility and governance capabilities under development.
- **User Access Management:** role-based permissions, connection limits and auditable controls under V7 qualification.
- **Risk governance:** execution, portfolio, capital, loss, recovery and emergency controls.
- **Standalone resilience:** an architectural requirement for Engines to retain risk controls while disconnected and reconcile safely on reconnect.
